Getting started takes you from an approved application to a workspace with your company details, your team, a default location, your Shopify store and a first product. The person who signs up becomes the workspace's admin and does most of the setup. The people they invite join as a Member or an Admin.

## How it fits together

<Diagram name="getting-started" caption="The first hour in Manuva, and who does each step" />

Steps 2 to 5 are admin steps: company, team and Shopify are under **Settings**, and warehouses are under **Locations** in the sidebar. Step 6 is on **Products**, and **Import Products** is open to members too. Do them in this order: a default location has to exist before you can create a retail item, and Shopify has to be connected before **Import Products** has anything to import.

## Getting an account

Manuva is in private beta, so a workspace starts from an application.

1. Open the **Apply for early access** page. The sign-in page links to it under "New to Manuva".
2. Fill in **Your name**, **Work email** and **Company name**. **Team size** and **What are you hoping to use Manuva for?** are optional. Click **Apply for access**.
3. Manuva shows "Thanks for applying. We'll review your application and email you when you're in." If that address has already applied, it shows "You already have an application on file — we'll be in touch soon."
4. When your application is approved, Manuva sends you a sign-up link. Open it. The page reads "Start your 30-day free trial" and "Full Pro-level access. No credit card required."
5. Check **Company name** and **Work email**, which are filled in from your application. Choose a **Password** of at least 8 characters.
6. Under **Plan after trial**, choose **Starter**, **Growth** or **Pro**, and **Annual (save ~20%)** or **Monthly**. This pre-selects that plan when the trial ends. The page shows what that plan allows.
7. Click **Start free trial**. Manuva creates your workspace, makes you its admin and opens the app. If your account needs email confirmation, Manuva shows "Check your email to confirm your account, then sign in to continue." instead, and the app does not open until you have confirmed and signed in.

<Screenshot src="/help/getting-started/signup.png" alt="The sign-up page with the company name and work email filled in, the plan chips and the Start free trial button" caption="Signing up from your invite link" />

During the 30-day trial you have Pro limits. A banner across the app counts the days: "[n] days left in your free trial." It links to **Upgrade now →**. When the trial ends, an admin is asked to pick a plan: the page says "Your free trial has ended" and "Pick a plan to keep using Manuva." The plan you chose at sign-up is pre-selected. These are the sign-up page's figures for each plan:

| Plan | Locations | Team members |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 1 | 3 |
| Growth | 5 | Unlimited |
| Pro | Unlimited | Unlimited |

The Locations pages need the Growth or Pro plan (or the trial), so a Starter workspace cannot create or choose a location, whatever the Locations figure says.

To come back later, use the sign-in page: enter your **Email** and **Password** and click **Sign in**.

## Setting up your company

*(admin)* Your company name, timezone, currency and logo live in one place. A new workspace starts on the timezone Pacific/Auckland and the currency AUD, so change them if you work somewhere else.

1. Click your initial or picture at the top right and choose **Settings**. Admins open on **Company**.
2. Check **Company name**.
3. Choose your **Timezone** from Pacific/Auckland, Australia/Sydney, Australia/Melbourne, Australia/Brisbane, Australia/Perth, Asia/Singapore, America/New_York, America/Los_Angeles, Europe/London, Europe/Berlin or UTC.
4. Choose your **Currency** from the options offered: AUD, EUR, GBP, NZD, SGD and USD.
5. Click **Save changes**. Manuva shows "Company settings updated".
6. To add a logo, go to the **Logo** card, choose a PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG file under 2 MB and click **Upload logo**. Manuva shows "Logo updated". The logo is used in exported reports.

<Screenshot src="/help/getting-started/company-settings.png" alt="The Company settings page with the company name, timezone and currency fields and the Logo card below" caption="Company settings" />

<Callout type="info">
Changing **Currency** only saves the setting. Manuva does not convert any amounts you have already entered.
</Callout>

The [Workspace admin](/help/workspace-admin) manual covers the settings pages in full.

## Inviting your team

*(admin)* Anyone you want in the workspace needs an invitation. You choose their role when you send it.

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. In the **Invite a member** card, enter their email address, choose **Member** or **Admin** in the role box, and click **Invite member**. The role starts as Member.
3. Manuva shows "Invitation sent to [email address]" and emails them: "[your name] invited you to [workspace] on Manuva", with an **Accept invitation →** button. The email says the invite expires in 7 days.

<Screenshot src="/help/getting-started/team-invite.png" alt="The Team settings page with the Invite a member card, a pending invitation and the member table" caption="Inviting a teammate" />

Until the invitation is accepted it appears under **Pending invitations**, with the columns **Email**, **Role**, **Expires** and **Actions**:

- **Resend** emails the same link again and starts a fresh 7 days.
- **Revoke** deletes the invitation.
- An invitation past its date shows "Expired [date]". Inviting the same address again sends a new link and resets the 7 days.

Accepted members appear in the table with **Name**, **Role**, **Status** and **Actions**. Change a person's role with the dropdown in their row. It saves as soon as you change it. Click **Deactivate** to remove someone's access. Manuva asks "Deactivate this member? They will lose access immediately." You cannot change your own role or deactivate yourself.

The roles you can give are:

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Member | Use the day-to-day screens. Settings shows them only **Profile** and **Appearance**. |
| Admin | Everything a member can do, plus the Workspace settings (**Company**, **Team**, **Integrations**, **Locations**, **Orders**, **Invoices**) and the admin-only actions in "Who can do what". |

### Accepting an invitation

The person you invited opens the email and clicks **Accept invitation →**. The page reads "Join [workspace] on Manuva" and "You've been invited as [role]. This invite is for [email address]."

- **No Manuva account yet.** Enter your **Full name**, a **Password** of at least 8 characters and **Confirm password**, then click **Accept invitation**. Manuva creates the account, signs you in and opens the app.
- **Account already exists.** If you are not signed in, the page says "An account already exists for this email. Sign in to accept this invite." Click **Sign in to accept →**. Once signed in as the invited address, the page says "You're signed in as [email address]. Tap the button to join." Click **Accept invitation**.

## Setting the default location

*(admin)* Manuva uses your default location in three places. The goods receipt form starts on it, and falls back to the first location in the list if there is no default. New retail items are filed against it, and a retail item cannot be created without one. Order allocation uses it too, and with no default location allocation does nothing. Nothing sets one for you: the first warehouse you add is not the default until you say so.

Locations are part of the Growth and Pro plans, and your trial. Without them the page says "Multi-location and bin management is available on Growth".

1. Click **Locations** in the sidebar, under Warehouse.
2. Click **Add Warehouse**, type a name in **Warehouse name** and click **Save**. Repeat for each warehouse.
3. Go to **Settings → Locations**. The page is titled **Default Location**.
4. Click **Set as default** next to the warehouse. It then shows a **Default** badge.

If you have no warehouse yet, the Default Location page says "No locations yet": "Create locations in the Locations module before setting a default." Its **Manage locations →** link goes to the Locations page. Sub-locations, aisles and bays are covered in [Inventory & warehouse](/help/inventory-and-warehouse).

## Connecting Shopify

*(admin)* Connecting Shopify lets Manuva read your products, variants and orders. It asks Shopify for read access to products and orders only. One workspace connects to one store here.

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**. The **Shopify** card shows **Not connected**.
2. On the Shopify card, click **Manage**.
3. Under **Shopify Connection**, enter your store address, for example your-store.myshopify.com, and click **Connect Shopify**.
4. Approve the access Manuva asks for on Shopify's own page. Finish in the same browser tab you started in, within 10 minutes.
5. Manuva returns you to **Integrations** with the message "Shopify store connected successfully." and the card shows **Connected**.
6. Click **Manage** again. Under **Connected stores** you see the store, **Last sync** and **Status**.
7. Optional: set a **Historical cutoff** date and click **Save**. The page explains: "Orders placed before this date are imported for stats only (no stock or planning). Re-sync after changing."
8. Click **Sync**. A "Sync in Progress" panel says "This may take a minute." When it finishes, Manuva shows "Shopify sync completed — [n] products, [n] orders synced."

<Screenshot src="/help/getting-started/integrations-shopify.png" alt="The Integrations page with the Shopify card open, showing a connected store, its last sync, the Historical cutoff date and the Sync and Disconnect buttons" caption="The Shopify card after connecting" />

Sync imports your products and the orders Shopify makes available to Manuva. Manuva asks Shopify only for read access to products and orders. The **Historical cutoff** marks orders placed before its date as stats only, with no stock or planning, once you sync again. When you connect, Manuva also registers Shopify webhooks for orders and products, so later changes in Shopify trigger a sync. If that step fails, Manuva shows "Store connected, but webhook registration failed. Check app URL and try reconnecting." You can still click **Sync** yourself.

To remove the connection, click **Disconnect** in the store's row. Manuva deletes its saved access to the store and shows "Shopify store disconnected." The connect box comes back under **Manage**.

If you install Manuva from the Shopify App Store instead, Shopify opens Manuva inside your store admin. If the store is not linked yet, click **Connect your Manuva account →**. A new tab opens on "Connect your Shopify store". Enter your **Email** and **Password** and click **Sign in and connect**. Manuva links the store to your workspace and takes you to your Shopify admin. Back in Shopify, refresh the Manuva page and click **Sync now**, or click **Open Manuva**.

Orders that arrive from Shopify are covered in [Orders & Shopify](/help/orders-and-shopify). To set up Xero, see [Setting up Xero](/help/purchasing-and-xero#setting-up-xero).

## Adding your first product

There are two ways to get a product into Manuva. Both start on **Products** in the sidebar.

**Bring in your Shopify catalogue.** Once Shopify is connected, click **Import Products** at the top of the page. Manuva shows "Synced [n] products and [n] orders." It runs the same sync as the **Sync** button on the Integrations page. Products you make from components then need a bill of materials: see [Products & BOMs](/help/products-and-boms).

**Add a retail item.** *(admin)* A retail item is a product you buy in and sell unchanged. It needs a default location first (see "Setting the default location").

1. On **Products**, click **New retail item**.
2. Enter the **Name**. It is the only required field. Optionally enter the **SKU**, **Barcode**, **Unit cost**, **Reorder point** and **Supplier**.
3. Click **Create**. Manuva opens the new item's page.

<Screenshot src="/help/getting-started/new-retail-item.png" alt="The New retail item dialog with name, SKU, barcode, unit cost, reorder point and supplier fields" caption="Adding a retail item" />

The dialog explains: "A product you buy in and sell unchanged. Manuva tracks its shelf stock and takes it off when an order is fulfilled."

## Statuses explained

| Status | What it means | What moves it on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not connected (Shopify card) | No active store is linked to the workspace. | **Connect Shopify** under **Manage**. |
| Connected (Shopify card) | At least one store is active. | **Disconnect**. |
| Status: ok (under Connected stores) | The last sync finished. | The next sync. |
| Status: failed (under Connected stores) | The last sync stopped with an error. | Fix the cause, then **Sync**. |
| Status: disconnected (under Connected stores) | The store was disconnected from Manuva. | Connect it again. |
| Status: unknown (under Connected stores) | The store has no sync recorded yet. | **Sync**. |
| active (Team table) | The person can sign in and use the workspace. | **Deactivate**. |
| deactivated (Team table) | The person has lost access. | Nothing on the Team page brings them back. |

## Who can do what

Settings is split in two. Every signed-in person gets **Personal** (**Profile**, **Appearance**). Only admins see the **Workspace** group. If a non-admin opens Company, Team, Integrations or Locations by address, Manuva sends them to **Profile**.

| Task | Member | Admin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open Company, Team, Integrations and Locations settings | No | Yes |
| Change company details and the logo | No | Yes |
| Invite, resend, revoke, change a role, deactivate | No | Yes |
| Connect or disconnect Shopify (the buttons are on the Integrations page) | No | Yes |
| Click **Import Products** on the Products page | Yes | Yes |
| Click **Sync** on the Integrations page | No | Yes |
| Add a warehouse | Yes | Yes |
| Set the default location | No | Yes |
| Create a retail item | No | Yes |

The **Import Products** and warehouse rows have no admin check in the code: any signed-in member can do them. The Shopify connect, sync and disconnect routes also check only that you are signed in with a workspace, so members are kept out only because the Integrations page is hidden from them. The Integrations and **Sync** rows are admin-only because only admins can open the Integrations page. Adding a warehouse also needs the Growth or Pro plan, or the trial.

`super_admin` counts as an admin. Platform support accounts (`platform_observer`) are read-only and are not admins.

## Common questions

- **Does the trial need a card?** No. The sign-up page says "Full Pro-level access. No credit card required."
- **What happens when the trial ends?** An admin is asked to pick a plan. The page says "Your free trial has ended" and "Pick a plan to keep using Manuva." The plan you chose at sign-up is pre-selected.
- **How long does an invitation last?** 7 days from when you send or resend it. Inviting the same address again sends a new link.
- **I was invited but I already have an account.** Sign in with the address the invitation was sent to, then click **Accept invitation**. A different account is refused.
- **Can I be in more than one workspace?** Yes. If you belong to more than one, the user menu shows a **Switch Tenant** box and a **Switch** button.
- **Why can't I open Locations settings?** The Locations pages are part of the Growth and Pro plans, and your trial.
- **Does Manuva import my old Shopify orders?** A sync imports the orders Shopify makes available to Manuva. Orders placed before the **Historical cutoff** date are imported for stats only, with no stock or planning. Sync again after changing the date.
- **Will Manuva stay in step with Shopify after the first sync?** Manuva registers Shopify webhooks when you connect, and a change to an order or product in Shopify triggers a sync. Click **Sync** if you want one straight away.
- **What does Disconnect do?** It deletes Manuva's saved access to the store and marks the store disconnected. Connect again from **Manage** when you want it back.
- **Why does Manuva open a scanner on my phone?** On a phone or tablet browser, the Dashboard sends you to the Warehouse Scanner page.
- **Do the Timezone and Currency settings change what I see?** The Company page saves them for your workspace. Changing the currency does not convert amounts you have already entered.
