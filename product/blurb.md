# Assemblio

**Manufacturing operations, without the chaos.**

Assemblio gives product businesses a single place to manage inventory, build orders, and stay on top of purchasing — so you spend less time chasing spreadsheets and more time making things.

---

## What it does

**Inventory that stays accurate.** Track stock across every bin and location in real time. Assemblio flags dead stock, low reorder points, and integrity issues before they become problems on the floor.

**Bills of materials that actually get used.** Build and version your BOMs, track component costs, and see margin at a glance. When a product changes, your production flow changes with it.

**Purchasing with full visibility.** Create purchase orders, track deliveries, and catch discrepancies the moment they arrive. Know what you're owed, what's overdue, and what it's all costing you.

**Warehouse operations from the ground up.** Manage locations, run stocktakes, and move inventory with confidence. Every movement is logged — nothing disappears into thin air.

**Reports that tell you what matters.** Inventory valuation, supplier lead time accuracy, spend by supplier, dead stock, PO variance — live data, not last week's export.

---

## Built for teams who make things

Whether you're fulfilling direct-to-consumer orders or supplying wholesale accounts, Assemblio handles the complexity behind the scenes — so your team can focus on output, not administration.
