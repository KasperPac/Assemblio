Part 2 — Competitive Positioning

Katana MRP — Primary Competitor

Their buyer
Shopify-native product brand, 5–50 person team, $500K–$5M revenue, has outgrown spreadsheets. Usually selling finished goods with a manufacturing step (apparel, supplements, food & beverage, cosmetics, hardware).
THEIR STRENGTHS
  • Shopify App Store presence — first result for manufacturing on Shopify
  • Visual drag-and-drop production scheduler
  • Established brand — 7+ years, many G2 reviews
THEIR WEAKNESSES
  • Add-on pricing: base + mfg + traceability = ~$807/mo
  • No capacity planning at any price point
  • No staff costing or time tracking
  • No yield % in BOMs
  • No BOM versioning — changing a BOM destroys history
  • No financial profitability dashboard
"Katana is a solid tool, but by the time you add the warehouse and traceability modules you need, you're paying $800/mo — and you still don't get capacity planning, BOM versioning, or the ability to track what each production run actually cost in labour. Manuva gives you all of that for $249/mo with unlimited users."
Best Manuva tier for this conversation: Growth ($249/mo)

Craftybase — Entry-Level / Upgrade Path

Their buyer
Solo maker or very early-stage handmade brand. Often selling on Etsy as primary channel. Annual revenue under $200K. Candles, skincare, jewellery, baked goods, small-batch food.
THEIR STRENGTHS
  • Entry price point ($20–$49/mo) — lowest cost in market
  • Purpose-built for artisan/batch workflows
  • Etsy + Shopify integration in one tool
THEIR WEAKNESSES
  • No purchase orders or supplier management
  • No receiving / goods inwards workflow
  • No team features — effectively single-user
  • No capacity planning or staffing
  • Scales poorly past the first hire
"Craftybase is where a lot of our customers start out. But once you're writing POs to suppliers, training a warehouse team, and needing to know your production capacity for the next month, Craftybase hits a wall. Manuva Starter is $99/mo and includes everything you need to run a growing manufacturing operation."
Best Manuva tier for this conversation: Starter ($99/mo)

MRPeasy — Full-MRP Alternative

Their buyer
Traditional small manufacturer (not Shopify-first), 10–200 employees, physical goods with complex routing, subcontracting, or quality compliance requirements.
THEIR STRENGTHS
  • Full MRP — master production scheduling, capacity planning
  • Quality control and subcontracting modules
  • End-to-end traceability across the full supply chain
THEIR WEAKNESSES
  • Per-user pricing — 10 people = $490/mo before extras
  • Not Shopify-native — orders don't drive production automatically
  • Complex to set up — built for ops managers, not e-com founders
  • Dated UI
"MRPeasy is powerful if you're running a traditional manufacturing operation with 30 people on the floor. If you're a Shopify brand that manufactures your own products, it's overkill — and at $49/user/mo you'll hit $500/mo the moment you give your team access. Manuva Pro is $499/mo flat for unlimited users."
Best Manuva tier for this conversation: Pro ($499/mo)

Cin7 Core — Established ERP Anchor

Their buyer
Mid-market multichannel business, $5M+ revenue, selling across Shopify + Amazon + wholesale B2B, with accounting integration requirements and possibly multiple warehouses.
THEIR STRENGTHS
  • 700+ integrations — connects to almost everything
  • Full ERP coverage including CRM, B2B portal, advanced WMS
  • Established vendor — perceived lower risk for finance leadership
THEIR WEAKNESSES
  • $349/mo minimum — enterprise budget for SMB features
  • Complex to implement — guided onboarding adds cost
  • Overkill for Shopify-first brands
  • Not manufacturing-first
"Cin7 is built for businesses that need an ERP across 10 sales channels and a B2B wholesale portal. If you're manufacturing for Shopify, you'll pay $349/mo minimum for a lot of features you'll never use. Manuva is built specifically for Shopify manufacturers — better production tooling, lower price, up and running in a day."
Best Manuva tier: Growth ($249/mo) for most, Pro ($499/mo) for larger teams

Summary

Manuva's strongest competitive position: Growth tier ($249/mo) against Katana. Comparable manufacturing depth, unlimited users, BOM versioning + yield %, and better operational reports — at less than a third of Katana's real cost with add-ons.

The two gaps that need to close before Manuva can win without a discount:
1. Accounting integration — the absence of Xero/QuickBooks sync is the most commonly cited veto by finance stakeholders.
2. Lot/batch tracking — required for regulated product categories and increasingly expected by DTC brands with any traceability requirement.

The module where Manuva has no competition: Capacity planning + departments + staffing + actual time tracking + staff costing at Pro ($499/mo flat, unlimited users). No direct competitor offers this combination. Strongest story in a "Manuva Pro vs MRPeasy" conversation.
