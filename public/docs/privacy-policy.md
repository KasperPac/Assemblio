## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

### 5.1 Default Retention Period

- **Active Accounts:** Personal information is retained while your account is active
- **Closed Accounts:** After account deletion or closure, personal information is retained for **12 months**
  - Supports resolution of billing disputes
  - Allows verification of historical transactions
  - Maintains audit trails for compliance
  - Enables recovery of deleted data if you change your mind within this period
- **After 12 Months:** Personal information is permanently deleted unless legal obligations require longer retention

### 5.2 Activity Logs

- **Retention Period:** 12 months from the date of the activity
- **Purpose:** Used for security audits, compliance investigations, troubleshooting, and user support
- **Access:** Limited to Manuva staff with a legitimate business need
- **Deletion:** Logs are automatically deleted after 12 months unless:
  - A legal dispute or investigation requires longer retention
  - A court order mandates preservation
  - An active security incident is being investigated

### 5.3 On-Request Deletion

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing privacy@manuva.app.

- **Response Time:** We will acknowledge your request within 10 business days and complete deletion within 30 days
- **Method:** Deletion is permanent and cannot be recovered
- **Exceptions:** The following data may be retained after deletion:
  - Activity logs (for 12 months, as detailed above)
  - Billing and invoicing records (for 5 years, for tax compliance)
  - Audit trails (required for compliance and dispute resolution)
  - Information required by legal obligation

**Data Portability:** Before deletion, you can request export of your data in a portable format (CSV, JSON, or similar). We will provide this within 30 days of your request at no cost.

### 5.4 Legal & Compliance Exceptions

Certain data is retained longer to comply with Australian laws:

- **Tax Records:** Retained for **5 years** per Australian Taxation Office requirements
- **Financial Records:** Retained per Australian Accounting Standards and business record requirements
- **Fraud Investigations:** Retained as needed to investigate and resolve fraud
- **Court Orders:** Retained indefinitely or as specified by court mandate
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Retained as required by government agencies or regulators

### 5.5 Automated Deletion

Manuva will automatically delete data after the applicable retention period expires, except where:

- Legal holds prevent deletion
- Disputes or investigations are ongoing
- The data has been aggregated and anonymized

You can monitor retention settings and deletion schedules in your account preferences.

## 7. Security & Data Protection

Manuva implements comprehensive security measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

### 7.1 Technical Safeguards

**Encryption:**

- **In Transit:** All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS)
- **At Rest:** Sensitive information (passwords, payment details, activity logs) is encrypted at the database level
- **Key Management:** Encryption keys are managed securely and rotated regularly

**Authentication & Access Control:**

- **Password Security:** Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with salt, not stored in plain text
- **Multi-Factor Authentication:** Available for high-security accounts
- **Session Management:** Sessions expire after a period of inactivity
- **Role-Based Access Control:** Users see only information their role is authorized to access

**Updates & Patching:**

- **Regular Patching:** All systems receive security updates within 48 hours of availability
- **Dependency Management:** Third-party libraries and dependencies are monitored and updated
- **Vulnerability Scanning:** Regular automated security scans and penetration testing

**Monitoring & Logging:**

- **Intrusion Detection:** Systems monitor for suspicious activity and unauthorized access attempts
- **Activity Logging:** All access to sensitive information is logged
- **Audit Trails:** Immutable records of data changes and system events

### 7.2 Organizational Safeguards

**Staff Training & Awareness:**

- All Manuva staff receive privacy and security training annually
- Confidentiality training covers handling of personal information
- Regular updates on privacy incidents and threat awareness

**Access Controls:**

- Personal information access is restricted to staff with legitimate business need
- Staff sign confidentiality agreements as a condition of employment
- Access to sensitive systems (databases, payment systems) requires approval from management
- Staff with access are required to use strong passwords and multi-factor authentication

**Privacy Audits:**

- Regular internal audits assess compliance with this Privacy Policy
- Third-party audits are conducted annually
- Findings are reviewed and remediated

### 7.3 Vendor Security

**Third-Party Security Requirements:**

- All service providers (Supabase, Stripe, Resend, Xero) maintain industry-standard security certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)
- Data Processing Agreements specify mandatory security obligations
- Vendors must notify us of security incidents within 24 hours
- We conduct regular security assessments of critical vendors

**Vendor Due Diligence:**

- Security questionnaires are completed during vendor onboarding
- Annual risk assessments are conducted for all major service providers
- Vendors with security incidents are escalated for review

### 7.4 Data Breach Response

**In the event of a data breach or suspected breach, Manuva will:**

1. **Immediately Investigate & Contain:** Identify the source of the breach, contain it, and prevent further unauthorized access
2. **Notify Affected Individuals:** Within 30 days, notify individuals whose personal information was compromised
   - **Notification includes:** Nature of breach, information involved, steps to protect against harm, and our response
   - **Communication channel:** Email and in-app notification
3. **Notify Regulators:** Report to the OAIC (Office of the Australian Information Commissioner) where required
4. **Provide Guidance:** Offer advice on protection measures (password changes, fraud monitoring, etc.)
5. **Document & Review:** Maintain records of the breach and conduct a post-incident review to prevent recurrence

### 7.5 Limitations

**No Guarantee of Absolute Security:**

While Manuva maintains robust security measures, we cannot guarantee absolute security. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. Manuva assumes no liability for unauthorized use, disclosure, or loss of data due to circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

**Your Responsibility:**

You are responsible for:

- Maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials
- Not sharing passwords with others
- Logging out of your account when using shared computers
- Reporting any suspicious activity immediately to privacy@manuva.app
- Keeping your personal contact information up to date

## 10. Definitions & Interpretations

This section defines key terms used throughout this Privacy Policy.

**Activity Log:** A record of user actions, system events, and interactions within Manuva, including timestamps, IP addresses, browser information, and descriptions of actions performed.

**Australian Privacy Principles (APPs):** Ten principles set out in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) that regulate how organizations in Australia handle personal information.

**Breach:** Unauthorized, unintended, or unlawful access to, disclosure of, loss of, or destruction of personal information.

**Data Controller:** An organization that determines the purposes and means of processing personal information. For manufacturer customers of Manuva, the customer is the controller; for suppliers and staff, Manuva is typically the controller.

**Data Processor:** An organization that processes personal information on behalf of a controller, according to the controller's instructions. Manuva acts as a processor for manufacturer customers' operational data.

**Data Subject:** An identified individual or individual who can be reasonably identified from information held. In the context of Manuva, this includes app users, supplier contacts, and staff members.

**Data Processing Agreement (DPA):** A contract between a controller and processor that specifies terms for processing personal information, including security obligations, permitted uses, and breach notification requirements.

**Encrypted:** Information converted to code using encryption algorithms and decryption keys so that it cannot be read without the key.

**OAIC:** The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, the independent federal privacy regulator for Australia.

**Personal Information:** Information about an identified individual or an individual who is reasonably identifiable. Includes name, email, phone number, address, activity records, and other identifying information.

**Privacy Act 1988 (Cth):** The primary Australian federal law governing privacy, which establishes the APPs and provides rights and obligations for personal information handling.

**Sensitive Information:** Information about health, race, ethnicity, political opinion, religion, philosophical belief, membership of a professional or trade association, trade union membership, sexual orientation, criminal record, or biometric information (or templates). Sensitive information requires higher levels of protection and stricter use limitations.

**Service:** The Manuva SaaS platform, website, applications, integrations, and associated services.

**Tenant:** Your organization's account and data space within Manuva, separate from other organizations.

**Third-Party Service Provider:** External organization providing services to Manuva (e.g., Supabase, Stripe, Resend, Xero) that processes personal information according to a Data Processing Agreement.
